# Sam Rose

## About

I'm a software engineer who loves building design systems and component libraries that empower teams to create beloved software products faster. My work lives at the intersection of design and engineering crafting accessible, well-architected UI infrastructure that scales across hundreds of teams while maintaining flexibility for individual product needs.

Currently, I'm a Senior Software Engineer at Shopify, where I work on Polaris, our design system used by 100+ internal teams and thousands of third-party developers. I focus on design system architecture, token systems, and driving adoption through tooling and documentation that makes the system truly flourish (and LLM friendly).

In the past, I've had the opportunity to build and scale design systems across different contexts from founding systems at startups to contributing to enterprise platforms at IBM and Indeed. I've driven adoption to 90%+ across Shopify Admin, led token system refactors supporting major redesigns, and built accessible component libraries serving millions of users globally.

When I'm not writing code, you can find me strength training, cooking healthy meals, exploring minimalist design, or reflecting on goals and systems thinking.
